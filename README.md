Run `npm run build` after editing source files, then `npm run check` to verify the generated pages and links.

The homepage's scrollable Research Updates list includes accepted/published papers from `data/publications.json` and patent announcements from `data/research-updates.json`. Submitted manuscripts from `data/ongoing.json` appear separately under Ongoing Work. Paper status and author roles come from those records; `venueDisplay` can override an abbreviated venue label. `data/research-updates.json` supplies dates with their available precision: publication years, journal issue periods, or conference dates, rather than inferred acceptance dates. Undated patent filings appear first, followed by dated entries from newest to oldest; year-only dates appear first within their year. Full-height text labels use muted violet for conference papers, teal for journal papers, and amber for patent applications, without decorative icons. First-author badges, summary counts, and filters always use the same muted blue, independently of the publication type; patent roles are omitted. Edit the template in `templates/home.html` and styles in `assets/publication-tools.css`, then commit the generated HTML together with the sources.

Research Update summaries link to the matching research on the homepage. Their footers contain only dates, linked to the paper's publisher page when available (or the patent page for patents). Date provenance stays in `source`; it is not used as a paper link. `linkToPaper: false` explicitly keeps TVCG's year unlinked. Undated entries have no date footer.

Publication cards and project citation details show verified volume, issue, pages, and special issues. Acceptance rates are displayed only for IEEE TVCG (16%, ISMAR 2026 journal track) and ACM UIST (20.1%), sourced from the author lab. Each `acceptanceRate` retains its scope and primary source in the data, while the page displays only the percentage without a link or parenthetical explanation. WHC 2023 and EuroHaptics rates are intentionally omitted at the author's request; the two regular IEEE ToH articles also have no displayed rate. Do not substitute an oral-presentation rate for the overall paper rate or a journal-wide estimate for a conference-linked special issue.
